BATTLE EMPIRES - DESIGN v3 (9 Oct 2026)
=========================================
Built from your research notes (Age of Empires I, League of Legends, WorldQuest,
SpellForce 3, March of Giants), plus a check of what players and critics actually say
about those three games.


WHAT THE RESEARCH SAYS
----------------------
WorldQuest (71% of 87 Steam reviews)
- Proved that fighting and building can share one team purse. Every purchase is a
  trade-off.
- Small and repetitive. It shows the idea can work, not how to make it big.

SpellForce 3
- Critics loved the hero, RPG and dungeon side.
- They disliked the RTS side: "toss the blob of paper-weak soldiers at enemies while
  using hero spells". It ends in deathballs, units have only "two modes", and the
  hero can win alone.
- Lesson: heroes must open the way, but armies and siege are needed to take a base.

March of Giants (playtesting now)
- The giant automatically rallies soldiers who follow it, so armies need no
  micromanagement. We copy this.
- Problems reported:
  * The building layer made lanes stall into drawn-out stalemates. Lesson: buildings
    should push the pace, not only defend.
  * Neutral objectives had too little impact.
  * Item power flattened out after 30 minutes.
  * A giant near an outpost could hold off several enemies.

Age of Empires and LoL
- Keep: villagers gather from a map that runs out, ages unlock content, LoL-style
  hero control, last hits, recall and the shop.
- Drop: four separate stockpiles. One team treasury makes every choice compete
  (research recommendation).

Battle for Middle-earth
- Players praise fighting over good build spots. Our version: rich mines are guarded
  by monster lairs.


THE GAME IN ONE SENTENCE
------------------------
Three heroes per side fight, hunt and claim the land. Every kill and every cleared
lair fills ONE shared war chest ("Supplies"). That chest pays for hero items, troops,
buildings and the next Age. The first side to break the other's city wins.


DECISIONS
---------
1. Two teams, three heroes each (3v3)
   - A team is one faction (Gondor, Rohan, Mordor or Isengard).
   - Its three players take that faction's three heroes, one each, with no repeats.
   - Empty slots are filled by bots.

2. One shared base per team, LoL-style diagonal map
   - Bases sit in the bottom-left and top-right corners.
   - Three roads (top, middle, bottom) run between them, each with forgotten towers
     to claim.
   - Two jungles, one per side, plus a contested middle with the Cave Troll lair.
   - Win by destroying the enemy Town Center, which stays open on all sides. You
     need an army or siege for it, because the Town Center defends itself
     strongly against heroes.

3. One currency: SUPPLIES, shared by the team
   - Everything costs Supplies: buildings, troops, Ages, research and hero items.
   - Every teammate can spend, and the HUD shows who bought what.
   - Income sources:
     * Villagers depositing loads. A rich mine pays more per load than a field.
     * Hero kills of enemy troops: a small reward always, bigger when a hero lands
       the last hit.
     * Hero kills of enemy heroes.
     * Jungle camps: large rewards.
     * Claimed forgotten towers: a trickle while held.
     * A small passive income.
   - Target from the research: about 60% of income should come from active hero play.

4. Hero-led economy
   - The richest resource sites (stone and iron mines) sit inside monster lairs.
     Villagers will not work a guarded site until a hero clears the lair.
   - The lair respawns after a while, so heroes keep returning to protect it.
   - Clearing camps and claiming towers is where the gold comes from.

5. Villagers fully automatic
   - Villagers pick the best safe site themselves, weighted by its value per trip.
   - The only control is a Shelter button.
   - The Steward (on by default) builds houses and the core buildings, so the
     heroes can stay out on the map. You can turn it off and build yourself.

6. Armies follow their hero (March of Giants)
   - Squads you train belong to you and march with your hero by default (Follow me).
   - Simple orders remain: hold, attack-move, march a road, return.
   - Counters from v2 stay (spear vs riders and so on), so armies don't become a
     deathball.
   - "Army loses heart" stays: a hero's own squads fight worse while that hero is
     dead.

7. Pace
   - Aim for 20-30 minute matches.
   - Buildings are offensive as well as defensive (siege works, forward camp) to
     avoid stalemates.
   - Ages need Supplies plus at least one cleared lair or claimed tower, so
     progress needs action, not just waiting.

8. Look: everything low-poly
   - Quaternius low-poly people (realistic proportions) for all soldiers and heroes,
     with CC0 weapons and faction colours.
   - Nature and village kits stay.


DEFERRED (next versions)
------------------------

- Elves and Dwarves factions.
- A campaign.
- Spending approval or reservations (for now a purchase feed is enough).
- Comeback bonuses (test first whether objectives already give enough comeback).
