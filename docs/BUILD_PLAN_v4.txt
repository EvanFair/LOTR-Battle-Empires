BATTLE EMPIRES - DETAILED BUILD PLAN v4 (9 Oct 2026)
=====================================================
Copy, don't invent. Every system below names the reference it comes from:

  [LoJ] League-of-Jinx (DaughterOfZaun, commit 4943358)
        A Godot 4.4 GDScript port of League of Legends' own scripts.
        Best source for HOW things are built: units, stats, buffs, spells, missiles,
        attack timing, the HUD widgets.
        Limits: an early prototype. One champion (Ahri), no minions, no items, no shop,
        no armour maths, and spatial queries are stubs. No licence and LoL art, so we
        port its patterns, not its files.

  [OMB] omoba-bevy (o-moba, v0.44)
        A complete, balanced MOBA with 17 heroes, items, jungle, bosses and towers.
        Best source for WHAT to build: hero kits and passives, numbers, crowd-control
        rules, items, objectives.
        Its art is CC-BY / CC0; we already use its icons and sounds.

  [BFME] Battle for Middle-earth, read through your mod files, with OpenSAGE's data
         tables (OpenBFME itself only reads the .big archives).
         Best source for the LOTR RTS layer:
           - hordes (our squads) with banner carriers, ranks and formations
           - hero leadership auras
           - hero power levels, the spellbook (team powers)
           - unit command buttons and stances
           - plot-based building
           - the Palantir HUD
           - all LOTR names, stats, models, portraits and sounds

Your decisions this plan follows:
- Personal use only, so BFME assets are fine to use.
- No army cards. Armies are selected and commanded like BFME / Age of Empires: click
  or drag-select, then a selection panel with portraits and command buttons.
- LoL-style HUD with the minimap bottom right. Your teammates (and the enemies you can
  see) show HP and level.
- Hero passives and combos copied from the MOBA references, plus hero <-> squad
  interactions from BFME.
- Characters and models come later, from your BFME upload (pipeline in Part F).


======================================================================================
PART A - ENGINE FOUNDATIONS (everything else builds on these)
======================================================================================

A1. Stats system  [LoJ engine/stats.gd + engine/game/balancer.gd]
  New file: source/lotr/combat/Stats.gd (one per unit).
  - Every stat = base + per_level * (level-1), then flat bonuses, then percent
    bonuses, then multiplicative percent bonuses.
  - Stats: max_hp, hp_regen, max_mana, mana_regen, attack_damage, ability_power,
    armour, magic_resist, attack_speed, attack_range, move_speed, crit_chance,
    lifesteal, tenacity, cooldown_reduction (ability haste), and damage_vs_troops,
    damage_vs_heroes, damage_vs_buildings.
  - A "temp" layer is cleared and rebuilt every 0.25 s from items, buffs, auras,
    research and level. This replaces today's scattered apply_buff / recompute_stats
    / speed_bonus / attack_speed_bonus fields.
  - Read-only getters (stats.armour, ...) are used everywhere. Old fields stay as thin
    shims until each user is migrated.
  - Units with stats: heroes, troops, villagers, creatures, buildings. Buildings only
    use hp, armour and damage.

A2. Status flags  [LoJ engine/status.gd]
  - Flags: can_move, can_attack, can_cast, can_be_targeted, stealthed, revealed,
    unstoppable, invulnerable, cc_immune, silenced, rooted, stunned, feared, taunted,
    charmed, airborne, grounded (no dashes), disarmed (no basic attacks).
  - Buffs set and clear the flags; nothing else writes them.
  - Every order and cast checks the flags. This replaces is_stunned() / is_rooted().

A3. Buff manager  [LoJ engine/buffs/buff.gd, buff_slot.gd, buff_manager.gd]
  New: source/lotr/combat/Buff.gd, BuffManager.gd, plus buffs/ with one script per
  buff.
  - Buff fields:
    * source unit, key, duration, stacks, max_stacks
    * type flags: STUN, ROOT, SLOW, SILENCE, FEAR, TAUNT, CHARM, KNOCKUP, DISARM,
      BLIND, GRIEVOUS, SHIELD, HASTE, AURA, DOT, MARK, STEALTH, UNSTOPPABLE
    * icon (from our EM status icons and AB ability icons), tooltip
  - Add types (copied verbatim from LoL via LoJ):
    * REPLACE_EXISTING
    * RENEW_EXISTING
    * STACKS_AND_RENEWS
    * STACKS_AND_CONTINUE (queued)
    * STACKS_AND_OVERLAPS (independent copies)
    * Per-source stacks option ("stacks_exclusive").
  - Hooks a buff can implement:
    on_activate, on_deactivate(expired), on_update_stats (feeds A1's temp layer),
    on_tick(dt), on_before_damage_taken, on_damage_dealt, on_hit (basic attack lands),
    on_kill, on_death, on_cast.
  - cleanse(types) removes negative buffs by type. Used by Théoden R and quicksilver-
    style items.
  - Replicated to clients as [key, source_id, stacks, remaining] in the slow state
    (2 Hz), so client HUDs show icons and timers.

A4. Damage pipeline  [LoJ Unit.apply_damage signal chain + OMB mitigation formula]
  - Combat.deal_damage(src, target, amount, type, source_kind, tags):
    * type: PHYSICAL, MAGIC, TRUE
    * source_kind: BASIC, SPELL, DOT, ITEM, TOWER, BUILDING
    * tags: aoe, siege, execute, no_lifesteal, ...
  - Order of operations:
    1. The attacker's on_damage_dealt hooks run (passives such as Aragorn's
       execute mark, Lurtz bonus vs rooted).
    2. Multipliers from the tag tables (hero AoE vs troops x1.5, siege vs buildings,
       troop counters, army heart).
    3. Mitigation: dmg * 100 / (100 + max(0, armour or MR - penetration)) [OMB].
       Negative armour amplifies: 2 - 100/(100 - armour). TRUE skips this.
    4. The target's on_before_damage_taken hooks run (Boromir block, Witch-king
       death-save, parry).
    5. Shields absorb next. One shield entry per source; re-casting from the same
       source stacks up to 2x; the earliest-expiring shield drains first [OMB].
    6. HP falls; damage numbers and kill credit follow.
    7. Lifesteal: basic attacks only, capped at 35% [OMB].
  - Kill credit: an assist window of 10 s per damaging or CC-ing enemy hero, and also
    for allies who healed or shielded you. Bounties follow D2.

A5. Crowd-control rules  [OMB crowd_control.rs + LoL tenacity]
  - Root: can still attack and cast; no movement or dashes. Takes the longer of the
    old and new duration.
  - Stun, fear, charm, taunt, knock-up: hard CC. A new hard CC overwrites the old one.
  - Slows: one entry per source; only the strongest applies, with a floor of 30% of
    base speed.
  - Tenacity (items, Boromir R, Uglúk W) shortens stun, root, slow, fear and charm.
    It does not shorten knock-ups (LoL rule).
  - Diminishing returns on heroes only: the same CC type within 6 s lasts 100%, then
    50%, then 25%, then the hero is immune for 3 s. Troops have none, so stuns melt
    armies.
  - Unstoppable: immune to CC, still takes damage. Spell shield: blocks the next
    ability (Arwen-style item later).

A6. Spells (abilities)  [LoJ engine/spells/spell.gd + ai_cast_state.gd]
  New: source/lotr/combat/Spell.gd (base class) and spells/<hero>/<Ability>.gd, one per
  ability. These replace HeroAbilities.gd's "kind" switch; generic kinds stay as base
  classes (StrikeSpell, NovaSpell, SkillshotSpell, DashSpell, ...) so per-hero scripts
  stay short.
  - State machine: READY -> CASTING (windup 0-0.8 s, can be cancelled by moving if the
    spell is cancellable) -> CHANNELING (optional) -> COOLDOWN.
  - Targeting: SELF, UNIT, POINT, DIRECTION (line), CONE, AREA, UNIT_OR_POINT,
    ALLY_UNIT, ALLY_OR_SQUAD (copied from OMB Frostguard: leap to an allied hero OR
    an allied squad).
  - Per-rank data: cooldown, mana, range, damage, AP/AD ratios, effect values.
    Rank formula from OMB: +10% damage per rank, -6% cooldown per rank, 3 ranks;
    the ultimate unlocks at level 6.
  - Recast windows [OMB Stormfist, Dawnweaver, Chainkeeper]: a spell can open a 3 s
    "recast" with a different effect and cost (for example Aragorn's Q2).
  - Ammo/charges (OMB Emberveil R: 3 charges). Toggles (Faramir bow/sword stance from
    OMB Wildspark's weapon toggle).
  - Cast checks: status flags, range (walk into range first; that already exists),
    mana, cooldown.

A7. Missiles and areas  [LoJ engine/spells/missiles/* + OMB aim/telegraph]
  New: source/lotr/combat/Missile.gd with subclasses TargetMissile (homing),
  LineMissile (skillshot, collides with the first unit or pierces), ArcMissile
  (lobbed, lands at a point) and ReturnMissile (boomerang, OMB Emberveil Q /
  Dawnweaver W).
  - Collision uses our SpatialGrid every physics tick. This is the part LoJ never
    finished.
  - Areas: GroundZone (lingering circle with tick damage or slow: Shelob's web,
    Saruman's fire) and Telegraph (warning circle or line shown to everyone,
    0.4-0.8 s before impact: Saruman R, troll slam).
  - Networking: the host sends a spawn RPC (kind, from, to, speed, owner). Clients
    simulate the flight visually; hits are host-only.

A8. Basic attacks  [LoJ ai_attack_state.gd]
  - LoL timing: attack_delay = 1 / attack_speed; windup = attack_delay * cast_pct
    (0.25-0.35 by hero).
  - A move order during windup cancels the attack (kiting). Ranged attacks become
    TargetMissiles, speed 19 [OMB].
  - Crit uses OMB's deterministic meter: each attack adds crit%, and every whole 1.0
    of meter crits for x1.75.
  - "On-hit" hooks fire when the hit lands (items, passives).

A9. Unit AI states  [LoJ data/ai/hero.gd]
  - States: IDLE, MOVE, ATTACK_MOVE, ATTACK (soft/hard), CAST, CHANNEL, FLEE (fear),
    FORCED_MOVE (taunt/charm), DEAD.
  - Acquisition scans run on timers (0.25 s), reusing our target-priority table.
  - The same states drive troops (with horde behaviour, C2) and bots.


======================================================================================
PART B - HUD AND INTERFACE (LoL layout, our Middle-earth art)
======================================================================================
Art: our UI kit (UI01-UI16), cut out with background removal and set up as 9-slice
textures. After your BFME upload, Palantir art and portraits via MappedImage crops (F4).
Fonts: Cinzel for headings and numbers, Alegreya Sans for body text (both OFL).
All widgets live in source/lotr/hud/widgets/ and LotrHud.gd only lays them out.
HUD scale setting: 75-125%.

B1. Champion panel (bottom centre)  [LoJ center.gd, left.gd, bar.tscn; OMB match_hud.rs]
  - Portrait: round frame UI16, an XP ring (TextureProgressBar, radial fill), a level
    badge, a death overlay with a respawn countdown, and the passive icon with a
    tooltip.
  - Stats column: icons plus numbers for attack damage, ability power, armour, magic
    resist, attack speed, move speed, crit and haste. Hovering a stat shows how it is
    built up.
  - Ability slots QWER [LoJ ui_spell.gd + clock.gd]:
    * TextureProgressBar with fill_mode = clockwise, using the icon as both under and
      progress textures, tinted.
    * Two sweep-edge lines drawn in _draw; a cooldown number (decimals under 1 s);
      a dark blue overlay when mana is too low.
    * Rank pips under the slot, and a gold "+" level-up button above when points are
      free.
    * A recast glow while a recast window is open; charge counter in the corner.
    * Key label. Ultimate slot uses the UI04 gold frame.
  - D / F hero utility slots, B recall slot, 5-8 item slots, all with the same sweep.
  - HP and mana bars: "991 / 1483" in the middle, regen at the right end ("+2.7").
    Shield shown as a white segment after the green, plus a red "recently lost" trail.
  - Buff row above the slots [LoJ ui_buff.gd]: icons with a radial duration fill and a
    stack count. Debuffs have red borders, buffs green.
  - Cast/channel bar above the panel while channelling (recall, Fire of Orthanc).

B2. Selection panel  [BFME Palantir + Age of Empires selection panel]
  - When you select squads, buildings or villagers (click or drag box), the champion
    panel's centre switches to the selection.
  - One squad (horde) selected:
    * Portrait in the frame with name, rank (1-3 stars, see C3), soldier count
      ("5/6"), banner-carrier icon and HP bar.
    * Formation buttons (C2), stance buttons (Aggressive / Defensive / Hold Ground),
      squad abilities (C5), Follow-me (G) and Return.
    * Upgrade buttons (forged blades and so on), greyed when not researched.
  - Several squads: a grid of small portraits with mini HP bars, as in Age of Empires.
    Click one to narrow the selection; Shift-click removes it. The command buttons
    shown are the ones they share.
  - A building: its portrait, HP, production queue with progress, train buttons, the
    rally point, and upgrade buttons.
  - Villager selection (rare, since they are automatic): Shelter and "work here"
    buttons.
  - Space or F1 snaps back to your hero, and the panel returns to the champion layout.
  - Control groups: Ctrl+1..9 to set, 1..9 to recall, double-tap to centre [AoE/BFME].
  - A floating info bubble over the selection, as in BFME: name, rank stars and an HP
    bar.

B3. Minimap (bottom right)  [LoJ minimap/*; OMB minimap.rs]
  - The map rendered top-down into a texture once, with fog of war multiplied over it.
  - Icons:
    * Heroes as their portraits in team-coloured rings.
    * Squads as dots.
    * Towers, lairs (with respawn timers), troll pits, city.
  - The camera frustum outline and your hero's path line [LoJ
    minimap_overlay_layer.gd]. Left click pans, right click moves.
  - Ping wheel on Alt+click: "On my way", "Danger", "Attack here", "Missing".
  - The frame is the UI10 art.

B4. Team frames (right edge)  [LoL; OMB enemy_portraits.rs]
  - The two allies: portrait, level, HP and mana slivers, ultimate-ready dot, respawn
    timer when dead. Click to centre the camera, Alt-click to ping.
  - Enemy heroes in the top-right strip: portrait greyed when out of sight, with
    their last-seen time and death timers.

B5. Scoreboard and clock (top right) + Tab overlay
  - Team kills "Free 7 - 4 Shadow", your K/D/A, last hits, game clock and FPS.
  - Holding Tab: a full table of all 6 heroes (level, K/D/A, last hits, items, total
    gold earned) and team powers.

B6. Announcements and kill feed (top centre)  [OMB tactical_hud.rs]
  - Banners: "First Blood", "Enemy slain", "Ally slain", "Shut down", "Double kill",
    "Tower claimed", "Lair cleared", "Troll slain", "Your city is under attack",
    "Age advanced".
  - Each shows the killer and victim portraits, with a sound sting.
  - The kill feed keeps the last 5 lines on the right under the scoreboard.

B7. Over-head bars  [LoJ healthbar.gd + meter.gd]
  - Heroes: name, level box, HP bar with a tick every 100 HP, mana sliver, shield
    segment, and a CC text flash ("Stunned").
  - Colours: you green, allies blue, enemies red.
  - Squads: one bar per squad over the banner (horde HP) with rank stars. Soldiers get
    no individual bars (BFME style), so it stays clean.
  - Buildings: wide bar with a construction %.
  - Creatures: neutral yellow.
  - Damage numbers [LoJ font_emitter]: white physical, blue magic, grey true, red
    damage taken, gold "+N" for last hits.

B8. Shop  [OMB shop.rs + LoL]
  - Opened with P at the city (or while the forge is active, OMB Cinderforge's
    rule).
  - Left: category tabs (Attack, Magic, Defence, Movement, Consumables).
  - Middle: an item grid with "Recommended for <hero>" first.
  - Right: the selected item's build tree (components -> item -> upgrades), with
    costs adjusted for owned parts [OMB: component cost fully credited], and
    Buy/Sell/Undo buttons.

B9. Kingdom panel (B key or the city button)
  - Tabs: Build (a radial menu on build plots, C6), Army, Age, Smithy, and Powers
    (spellbook, C7).
  - Steward and Shelter toggles.

B10. Tooltips  [LoL]
  - Name, key and rank on the first line; cost, cooldown and range on the second.
  - Description with coloured keywords: physical orange, magic blue, true white,
    heal green, shield light grey, crowd control purple, stealth grey. Scaling in
    brackets, for example "(+60% AD)".
  - Shift shows the full numbers for every rank.

B11. Cursors and feedback
  - Cursors from UI15: default, attack (sword), ally (hand), cast (target ring),
    shop and build.
  - Range indicators, aim shapes, move/attack markers and recall ring already exist;
    restyle them to the art set.


======================================================================================
PART C - ARMIES THE BFME WAY (hordes, leadership, ranks, formations)
======================================================================================
C1. Hordes = our squads  [BFME HordeContain]
  - A squad becomes a horde object with ranks of soldiers in fixed slots (RankInfo
    positions) and a banner carrier once it reaches rank 2.
  - Formation is kept while moving: soldiers move to their slot relative to the
    banner. One selection and one HP bar for the whole horde; individual soldiers die.
  - Reinforce: a horde standing in its own city refills missing soldiers for a
    Supplies cost.

C2. Formations and stances  [BFME HORDE_TOGGLE_FORMATION, TOGGLE_STANCE]
  - Infantry: Line (default), Shield Wall (+armour, -speed), Wedge (charge, +damage
    on the first hit).
  - Spearmen: Pike Wall, which stops cavalry charges (C4).
  - Archers: Spread (less AoE damage taken) and Fire Arrows (needs an upgrade; extra
    damage to buildings).
  - Cavalry: Charge (trample infantry; a knock-back that stuns for 0.5 s).
  - Stances: Aggressive (chase), Defensive (hold an area), Hold Ground (don't move,
    +armour).

C3. Ranks and experience  [BFME ExperienceLevel]
  - Hordes earn XP from kills and from fighting near their hero.
  - Rank 2: banner carrier, +10% HP. Rank 3: +15% damage, gold trim.
  - Heroes also gain XP from their hordes' kills (shared).

C4. Unit counters (kept, BFME numbers)
  - Spears beat cavalry, cavalry beats archers, archers beat infantry, infantry beats
    spears.
  - Siege beats buildings, heroes are strong against small hordes, and cave trolls
    crush infantry [BFME CrusherLevel].

C5. Hero <-> squad interactions  [BFME leadership modules + OMB ally-target spells]
  - Leadership auras (BFME ModifierList): each hero has an aura for the friendly
    hordes around it.
    * Aragorn: +25% damage.
    * Boromir: +25% armour for Gondor infantry.
    * Théoden: +20% speed for Rohan cavalry.
    * Gothmog: +20% damage for orcs.
    * Uglúk: +20% armour for Uruk-hai.
    * Saruman: +10% damage for every Isengard unit, map-wide.
  - Ally-squad targeting [OMB Frostguard leap, Stormfist anchor]:
    * Boromir's E leaps to an allied squad and shields it.
    * Éomer's E (Charge) pulls his cavalry hordes with him.
  - Souls [OMB Chainkeeper]: Gothmog gains stacks from any unit dying within 15 m
    (+0.5 armour, +0.2 damage each).
  - Summons are real hordes that obey the selection panel (Army of the Dead, Rangers
    of Ithilien, Riders of the Mark, Warg Pack, Uruk Ambush).
  - Combo hordes [BFME ComboHorde]: two allied hordes of the right types merge into a
    stronger mixed horde.
    * Gondor soldiers + Gondor archers = Tower Guard.
    * Rohan spearmen + Westfold bowmen = Rohan Levy.
    * Orc warriors + orc archers = Mordor Rabble.
    * Uruk pikes + crossbows = Uruk Phalanx.
  - "Army loses heart" stays: hordes near their dead hero are weakened.

C6. Building on plots  [BFME CastleBehavior + BASE_FOUNDATION radial menus]
  - The city gets fixed build plots around the Town Center (like BFME 1). Click a plot
    to open a radial menu of buildings.
  - Forgotten towers and lairs get outpost plots once claimed: forward barracks, a
    well (healing) or a supply camp.
  - Walls and gates keep today's drag placement.

C7. Team powers (spellbook)  [BFME PlayerTemplate SpellBook + Science points]
  - Power points come from hero kills, lair clears and tower claims (our "feats").
  - Each faction has a tree of about 10 powers in 3 tiers, for example:
    * Gondor: Rally, Healing, Elven Allies, Army of the Dead (ultimate).
    * Mordor: Eye of Sauron, Darkness, Fell Beast strike, Balrog (ultimate).
    * Rohan and Isengard: their equivalents.
  - Each power has a long cooldown; the team picks from them together. This is
    BFME's best feature and fits our shared team economy.

    stuns it for 1.25 s, then 7 s immunity (OMB Concussion). He also blocks the first
    hit every 8 s.
  * Q Shield Bash: stun.
  * W Horn of Gondor [BFME]: enemy troops nearby flee for 2 s; allied hordes get +25%
    armour.
  * E Shield Leap: to an ally hero or squad; shield + 25 defence (OMB).
  * R Last Stand: unstoppable, and cannot die for 3 s.
- Faramir, marksman  <= OMB Wildspark
  * Passive "Ranger's Eye": after a takedown, +40% move speed and +50% attack speed
