BATTLE EMPIRES - PLAN v4 (9 Oct 2026)
=======================================
Focus of this version: a League-of-Legends-quality HUD, and deep hero gameplay (how
abilities work with and against each other). Character art waits for your Battle for
Middle-earth mod assets.

   - Battle for Middle-earth models, textures and sounds belong to EA (and the
     film/Tolkien licence holders), even inside free mods. Mod authors can share them
     with game owners, but we cannot put them in a public GitHub repo or a public
     download.
   - Options when you have it:
     (1) Use it only as reference (layouts, unit lists, ability ideas, map shapes) and
         keep our own CC0 art. Safe.
     (2) Keep the repo and releases private, for personal play only.
     (3) Only use parts the mod authors made themselves AND that they released under
         an open licence (rare; it has to be checked file by file).
   - Recommended: (1). Map ideas, unit rosters and ability designs are free to use;
     the files are not.

c) The "Tests failed" email

2. HERO GAMEPLAY: HOW ABILITIES WORK WITH AND AGAINST EACH OTHER
----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Core combat rules (new or reworked)
- Damage types:
  * PHYSICAL (swords, arrows), reduced by Armour.
  * MAGIC (Saruman, the Witch-king, Théoden's holy light, Morgul), reduced by Magic
    Resist.
  * TRUE (executes, Éowyn's R against the Witch-king), not reduced.
  * Items and Blacksmith research give Armour and Magic Resist, so building against
    the enemy matters.
- Crowd control (CC) taxonomy, shared by heroes, troops and creatures:
  * Hard CC: stun, knock-back/knock-up, fear (runs away), taunt, silence (no
    abilities).
  * Soft CC: slow, root (can walk no further, but can still attack and cast), weaken
    (deals less damage), blind (basic attacks miss), Morgul wound (healing -50%).
  * Tenacity (from items and Boromir's Last Stand) shortens CC.
  * Diminishing returns: a second stun within 4 seconds lasts half as long, a third
    a quarter. No chain-stun-locks.
  * Unstoppable windows (Last Stand, Bloodlust, charges): immune to CC while they
    last.
- Abilities become real projectiles:
  * Skillshots travel at a speed and can be dodged.
  * Ground AoEs show a telegraph circle before they land (about 0.4 s).
  * Enemies see the warning too, so dodging becomes a skill.
- Channels (recall, Saruman's Fire of Orthanc, Dread Shriek charge) are interrupted
  by hard CC.
- Stealth (Faramir W, Shelob E):
  * Invisible to enemies unless within 3 m of an enemy watchtower or claimed tower.
  * Revealed for 2 s when damaged.
  * The first attack out of stealth is empowered.
- Shields (Boromir's horn, Théoden's Arise Riders): absorb damage first, shown white
  on the HP bar.
- Passives (new, one per hero): a defining trait that rewards the hero's playstyle
  (see 2.3).

2.2 Heroes vs armies (so neither replaces the other)
- Hero AoE deals +50% to troops (heroes cleave squads). Single-target abilities do
  normal damage to troops.
- Squads deal +25% to heroes below level 6 and normal damage after that. "Army loses
  heart" stays.
- Squads within 8 m of their own living hero get +10% damage (the hero's presence).
  Your squads already follow you.
- Charges:
  * A mounted charge or leap knocks infantry back.
  * SPEARS stop it: a charge that hits a spear squad stuns the charger for 1 s.
  * Cavalry beats archers, spears beat cavalry, swords beat spears.
- Fear (the Witch-king's Dread Shriek, Gothmog's war cry) makes troops flee for a few
  seconds. Heroes are only slowed by it.
- Healing reduced by Morgul wounds, so Mordor counters Rohan's sustain.
- Buildings: only siege troops and abilities tagged "siege" do full damage. Heroes
  do 50%. The Town Center hits heroes harder than troops (Nexus-style turret rules),
  so you need an army to take a city.

2.3 The 12 heroes in 3v3 (each faction is one team of 3)
GONDOR (fighter / tank / marksman)
- Aragorn
  * Passive "Heir of Isildur": +5% damage per nearby allied hero or squad (max 3).
  * Q Andúril Strike: execute.
  * W For Frodo!: rally.
  * E Ranger's Dash.
  * R Army of the Dead: the summons FEAR troops they hit.
- Boromir
  * Passive "Shield of Gondor": blocks the first hit every 8 s.
  * Q Shield Bash: stun.
  * W Horn of Gondor: SHIELD for allies plus taunts nearby enemy troops.
  * E Charge: knock-back.
  * R Last Stand: unstoppable, and cannot die for 3 s.
- Faramir
  * Passive "Ranger's Eye": sees stealthed enemies within 6 m.
  * Q Volley: telegraphed.
  * W Ithilien Stealth.
  * E Precise Shot: root.
  * R Rangers: summon.
  * Combo: Boromir stuns, Faramir roots, Aragorn executes.

ROHAN (support / fighter / assassin)
- Théoden
  * Passive "Lord of the Mark": allied cavalry +10% speed.
  * Q Herugrim: MAGIC damage.
  * W Arise, Riders: shield + heal.
  * E Snowmane.
  * R Ride of the Rohirrim: team haste and CC cleanse.
- Éomer
  * Passive "Marshal": his charges pull his squads along.
  * Q Spear Throw: slow.
  * W Riders of the Mark.
  * E Charge: knock-back.
  * R Death!: rally.
- Éowyn
  * Passive "Shieldmaiden": a short shield after every dash.
  * Q execute.
  * W Shield Wall.
  * E Dernhelm: stealth dash.
  * R I Am No Man: TRUE damage, and triple against the Witch-king and Nazgûl.
  * Combo: Théoden hastes, Éomer charges in, Éowyn finishes.

MORDOR (tank / mage / assassin)
- Gothmog
  * Passive "Lieutenant of Morgul": orc squads near him +10% armour.
  * Q Cleave.
  * W Warg Pack.
  * E The Age of Men Is Over: FEAR on troops, slow on heroes.
  * R Siege Fury: siege tag for nearby troops.
- Witch-king
  * Passive "No Man Can Kill Me": survives a lethal hit once every 90 s (doesn't
    work against Éowyn).
  * Q Morgul Blade: Morgul wound plus damage over time.
  * W Black Breath: weaken.
  * E Fell Beast: leap.
  * R Dread Shriek: fear, then stun.
- Shelob
  * Passive "Venom": basic attacks poison.
  * Q Sting: stun.
  * W Web: root zone.
  * E Lurk: stealth leap.
  * R Feast: heal on kill, bigger on heroes.
  * Weakness: the Phial of Galadriel item makes her flee.
  * Combo: Shelob webs, the Witch-king shrieks, Gothmog cleaves the frightened army.

ISENGARD (tank / mage / marksman)
- Uglúk
  * Passive "Fighting Uruk": +attack speed below 50% HP.
  * Q Cleave.
  * W Bloodlust: unstoppable.
  * E Leap.
  * R Uruk Charge.
- Saruman
  * Passive "Voice": abilities silence for 0.5 s.
  * Q Fireball: telegraphed.
  * W Voice of Saruman: stun.
  * E Staff Blast: knock-back.
  * R Fire of Orthanc: channelled, long range.
- Lurtz
  * Passive "Hunter": +damage to targets that are slowed or rooted.
  * Q Heavy Arrow: root.
  * W Volley.
  * E Hunter's Stride.
  * R Uruk Ambush.
  * Combo: Saruman stuns, Lurtz pins, Uglúk dives.

Matchup notes (Free vs Shadow):
- Rohan's healing is countered by Morgul wounds.
- Gondor's armour stacking is countered by Saruman's magic damage.
- Mordor's fear is countered by Théoden's cleanse.
- Stealth (Shelob, Faramir) is countered by watchtowers and Faramir's eye.

2.4 Items (rework to LoL depth)
- About 24 items with components and build paths:
  * Basic (300) -> mid (800) -> legendary (2500).
  * Types: physical, magic, tank, support, boots.
- Every legendary has one unique effect, for example:
  * Andúril shards: execute range +.
  * Mithril: tenacity + armour.
  * Elven cloak: stealth on recall.
  * Morgul hilt: grievous wounds.
  * Phial: fear Shelob, reveal stealth.
- The shop shows "Recommended" per hero.
- Items cost team Supplies, so buying for your hero competes with the army.

2.5 Objectives and pacing (from v3, sharpened)
- Lairs: the mine opens, plus a feat.
- Troll pits: a team buff (the Troll's Strength) plus a big Supplies reward.
- Forgotten towers: vision, a Supplies trickle and a feat.
- Siege: the city needs troops. Siege works give rams and catapults.
- A late-game event (around minute 18): "The Eye turns" or "The Beacons are lit".
  It's a neutral super objective that grants a decisive army buff, so games end
  instead of stalling (March of Giants' complaint).
