BATTLE EMPIRES - DESIGN v2 (your answers, 8-9 Oct 2026)
=========================================================

MATCH
- No time limit. A match runs roughly 30-45 minutes.
- Win by destroying the enemy Town Center ("the city"). Like Age of Empires, a well
  laid-out town (walls, gates, towers) makes that much harder.
- Keep 4 bases.

THE MAP (next big change)
- Town Centers move about a third of the way in from the corners, open on every side:
  no mountain or map edge behind a base, so you can be attacked from any angle.
  The Town Center position is the only fixed thing in a base.
- No fixed lanes. Open fields joined by winding roads (base to base, to forgotten
  towers, to jungle camps). Units can walk anywhere; roads are the natural routes.
- FORGOTTEN TOWERS: neutral ruined towers along the roads. Stand next to one with your
  hero (no enemy nearby) for about 10 s to claim it; it then shoots for you. Enemies
  take it back the same way, or destroy it so it falls back to ruins.

ARMIES
- Only the squadrons you pay for (no free minion waves).
- Auto-repeat squadrons still march steadily ("minions walking"): per building you pick
  a target (an enemy base, a forgotten tower or a jungle camp) and they follow the
  roads there, fighting what they meet.
- You can send squads to clear jungle camps.
- Command from anywhere: box-select, right-click, G = follow me (built 9 Oct).
- If your hero is dead, your army loses heart: your squads deal less damage until the
  hero respawns.

HEROES
- Hero vs squadron depends on hero rank: hero LEVEL matters most (a low-level hero can
  be killed by a squad, a high-level hero cuts through squads), and LEGEND heroes
  (Aragorn, the Witch-king, Saruman...) start a little stronger than CAPTAINS
  (Lurtz, Gothmog...).

TOWN AND DEFENSES
- Bases start open. You drag-place walls (palisade, later stone).
- Where a wall crosses a road into your town, a gate is made automatically. At least
  3 roads lead into every town, so there are always at least 3 gates.
- Buildings can go anywhere (built 9 Oct).

VILLAGERS
- Far fewer villagers, fully automatic. One focus button: Balanced / Food / Wood /
  Stone / Iron. Villagers split themselves, leaning towards the focus.

LOOK
- Low-poly, adult proportions, closer to the real thing than cartoon (art prompts in
  docs/ART_PROMPTS.txt; your LowPoly asset pack to come via a GitHub release).

LAN (your question: "every interaction just dots and actions?")
- Yes, that is already how it works, and how big games do it:
  * Each player only sends COMMANDS (move here, attack that, cast Q) to the host.
  * The host runs the simulation and sends back compact state: unit id, position,
    health (10 times a second) plus slower info twice a second.
  * Every computer draws all the 3D graphics, animations, effects and sound itself.
    No graphics ever go over the network.
- League of Legends works the same way with a dedicated server. Age of Empires goes
  further ("lockstep"): it sends ONLY the commands and every PC runs an identical
  simulation. That uses the least network, but any tiny difference between PCs
  desyncs the game, so it is hard to do reliably in Godot.
- Planned improvements for us: only send units that changed, smaller numbers
  (16-bit positions), and don't send enemy units a player can't see (also stops
  map-hack cheating).
