Art direction

The target is the low-poly look of your reference images (adult proportions, faceted flat-shaded meshes, solid colours), dressed for a League of Legends style top-down camera.

  • ShapeFaceted low-poly, flat-shaded, visible facets. Heroic, slightly exaggerated proportions: broad shoulders, big hands and feet. Adults, never chibi.
  • ColourMuted earthy base, one strong faction accent. No photo textures or noise. Characters sit one step brighter than the ground so they pop.
  • LightWarm key light, cool shadows, soft ambient occlusion, light fog in the distance.
  • CameraTop-down MOBA camera at about 55°. Silhouettes, weapons and banners are oversized so they read from up there.
  • Team colourFaction colours carry identity. A small team-colour band (cape trim, banner, base ring) shows the player, since two players can pick the same faction.
  • 3D budgetTroops 3–5k triangles, heroes 8–12k, buildings 2–6k. One texture atlas per model. Rigged humanoids in glTF/GLB, Y-up, about 1.8 m tall.
  • OriginalityInspired by Tolkien's books. No character should resemble a film actor, so prompts say so.
  • 2D iconsSame low-poly look, rendered as a single subject on a dark ground. Ability icons must read at 64 px, emblems at 32 px.
